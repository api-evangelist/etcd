SIG-etcd shipped routine patch releases v3.5.32 and v3.6.13 addressing dependency CVEs, a websocket authentication bug fix, and a new option to help operators complete the v2 storage deprecation. Users on v3.5 and v3.6 should schedule updates during maintenance windows.
